Big Mac Salad

KetoMain
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 tsp sea salt & 0.25 tsp black pepper
  • 8 oz romaine lettuce
  • 1 cup chopped tomatoes
  • 0.75 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Dressing: 1/2 cup mayo, 2 tbsp diced pickles, 2 tsp mustard, 1 tsp white vinegar, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1.5 tbsp Erythritol

Instructions

  1. 1. Cook ground beef in a skillet over high heat. Season with sea salt and pepper (7-10 mins).
  2. 2. Puree dressing ingredients in a blender. Thin with water if needed.
  3. 3. Combine salad ingredients, add beef, and toss with dressing.

Alfredo Sauce

PastaSauce
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup butter
  • 1.5 cups heavy whipping cream
  • 2 tsp garlic minced
  • 0.5 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 cups freshly grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. 1. Add butter and garlic to skillet over low heat until softened.
  2. 2. Whisk in cream, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer.
  3. 3. Whisk in Parmesan until melted and serve.

Chickpea & Zucchini Burgers

VegetarianGluten-Free
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 can (540 mL) chickpeas, rinsed and dried
  • 2 medium zucchini (grated & squeezed dry)
  • 0.5 cup gluten-free flour + 0.5 cup GF breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cloves garlic minced, 2 tbsp red onion, 1 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp oregano

Instructions

  1. 1. Mash chickpeas thoroughly in a bowl.
  2. 2. Combine with zucchini, binder, egg, aromatics, and spices. Rest mix 5 mins.
  3. 3. Shape into patties and fry over medium heat 4-5 mins per side.
