Big Mac Salad

KetoMain
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 tsp sea salt & 0.25 tsp black pepper
  • 8 oz romaine lettuce (or iceberg)
  • 1 cup chopped tomatoes
  • 0.75 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 0.5 cup diced pickles
  • Dressing: 1/2 cup mayo, 2 tbsp diced pickles, 2 tsp mustard, 1 tsp white vinegar, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1.5 tbsp Erythritol

Instructions

  1. 1. Cook ground beef in a skillet over high heat with sea salt and pepper (7-10 mins).
  2. 2. Puree dressing ingredients in a blender. Thin with water if needed.
  3. 3. Combine salad ingredients, add beef, toss with dressing and serve.

Potato Salad

VegetarianSide
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs potatoes, cubed and boiled
  • 0.5 cup diced red onion
  • 1 cup diced dill pickles
  • 3 hard boiled eggs
  • 1.5 cups Miracle Whip
  • 1 tbsp mustard & 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp celery seed, salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. 1. Boil and cool cubed potatoes.
  2. 2. Mix Miracle Whip, mustard, vinegar, and spices in a large bowl.
  3. 3. Fold in potatoes, eggs, red onion, and dill pickles. Chill before serving.

Alfredo Sauce

PastaSauce
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup butter
  • 1.5 cups heavy whipping cream
  • 2 tsp garlic, minced
  • 0.5 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 cups freshly grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. 1. Add butter and garlic to skillet over low heat until softened.
  2. 2. Whisk in cream, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer.
  3. 3. Whisk in Parmesan until melted and serve immediately.

Braised Short Ribs

BeefMain
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 rack short ribs
  • 3 carrots & 5 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 large onions, sliced & 6 cloves garlic
  • 1 can tomato paste & splash of soy sauce
  • 2 cups chicken stock & 2 cups red wine (or amber ale)
  • 0.5 cup oyster sauce, fresh rosemary and parsley

Instructions

  1. 1. Season and brown ribs on all sides in tallow/oil. Set aside.
  2. 2. Saute onions, tomato paste, carrots, celery, and garlic.
  3. 3. Add liquids, nestle ribs back in, cover and bake at 275°F for 4-5 hours.

Chickpea & Zucchini Burgers

VegetarianGluten-Free
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 can (540 mL) chickpeas, rinsed and dried
  • 2 medium zucchini (grated & squeezed dry)
  • 0.5 cup GF flour + 0.5 cup GF breadcrumbs

Egg Roll in a Bowl

KetoMain
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground pork or beef
  • 14 oz shredded coleslaw mix
  • 0.25 cup soy sauce, 1 tsp ginger, 2 tsp sriracha
  • 1 whole egg, 1 tbsp sesame oil, green onions

Instructions

  1. 1. Brown pork/beef with garlic. Add coleslaw mix, soy sauce, and ginger.
  2. 2. Make a well in center, add egg and scramble.
  3. 3. Drizzle with sriracha, sesame oil, and green onions.

Shepherd's Pie

BeefMain
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs ground beef
  • 1 onion & 2 carrots (grated), 4 cloves garlic
  • Worcestershire sauce, oyster sauce, tomato paste, thyme, rosemary, red wine, stock
  • Mashed Potatoes: 2.5 lbs potatoes, 2 egg yolks, cream, butter, parmesan

Instructions

  1. 1. Cook beef with carrots, onions, garlic, tomato paste, wine, and stock.
  2. 2. Boil, mash, and mix potatoes with egg yolks, butter, and parmesan.
  3. 3. Layer meat, top with potatoes and parmesan, and bake at 350°F for 20 mins.

Spinach Stuffed Chicken

KetoChicken
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 0.25 cup Parmesan & 2 tbsp mayo
  • 1.5 cups chopped fresh spinach, garlic, paprika

Instructions

  1. 1. Slice a pocket into each chicken breast and season outside.
  2. 2. Mix cream cheese, parmesan, mayo, spinach, and garlic.
  3. 3. Stuff chicken breasts and bake at 375°F for 25-30 minutes.
